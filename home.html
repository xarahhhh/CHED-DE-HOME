Chicken Curry

Click to view full recipe

Cook onions in oil until golden, add ginger garlic paste, then chicken and cook till lightly browned. Add tomatoes and spices, cook until soft. Mix in yogurt, add water, cover and simmer for 20–25 minutes. Finish with garam masala and fresh coriander. Serve hot with rice or roti.
